# Problem Statement

Industrial workplaces (factories, warehouses, construction sites, and energy facilities) face critical risks from unaddressed safety hazards. Standard manual paper-based or ticketing reporting suffers from severe shortcomings:

1. **Slow Triage Delays:** Incidents reported on paper or generic ticketing queues take days to reach the correct safety officer.
2. **Subjective Risk Ratings:** Employees rate hazards inconsistently without a standardized [[Risk Scoring Rubric]].
3. **Lack of Immediate Compliance Guidance:** Workers frequently don't know the exact standard rule or corrective remediation required.
4. **Poor Audit Visibility:** Resolution tracking often lacks an immutable audit trail.
