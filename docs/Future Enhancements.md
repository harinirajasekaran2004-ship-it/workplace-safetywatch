# Future Enhancements

The following roadmap items represent planned expansions for Workplace SafetyWatch:

1. **Continuous Video RTSP Ingestion:**
   - Real-time video frame sampling from CCTV security cameras in warehouse corridors to autonomously flag blocked egress and missing PPE without requiring manual employee uploads.
2. **Edge Computer Vision Sidecars:**
   - Deploying lightweight edge YOLOv10 / TensorRT models on NVIDIA Jetson devices on the shop floor for sub-100ms slip detection.
3. **Automated Regulatory Filing Export:**
   - Exporting generated incident reports directly into OSHA 300 logs or company EHS (Environmental Health and Safety) SAP/Maximo enterprise systems.
4. **SMS / WhatsApp Two-Way Chat:**
   - Enabling employees to send hazard photos directly via WhatsApp or Twilio SMS gateway, with instant multi-agent response links.
5. **Spatial Digital Twin Mapping:**
   - Plotting incidents onto 3D CAD/BIM floorplans to identify safety risk heatmaps across the facility.
